The Gerald Idea Visualizer

A Private Garden for Growing Ideas

Ideas are fragile things. They begin as half-formed thoughts, vague stirrings in the margin of attention, and most of them die there—trampled by the next notification, the next meeting, the next day. The Gerald Idea Visualizer is an attempt to give them soil.

A personal, privacy-first workspace for capturing ideas and discovering the connections between them—using AI that never leaves your garden.

The Problem

We accumulate ideas the way a desk accumulates paper—into a growing, unmanageable pile. A note-taking app holds the fragments, but the relationships between them remain invisible. You can tag and file, but the act of connecting ideas is left to manual labor and fading memory. The result is a collection of notes that never becomes a system of thought.

Existing tools either demand too much structure upfront—force you into a rigid ontology before you even know what you're thinking—or they offload your ideas to cloud services whose incentives are fundamentally misaligned with your own. Your insights become training data, your patterns become product features, your private mental model becomes a marketing opportunity.

Key Concepts

Local AI Analysis

All analysis runs on the user's own server. Extractive summaries, embedding comparisons, and relationship scoring stay entirely local. Nothing leaves your garden.

GitHub as Source of Truth

Ideas live in a GitHub repository as plain markdown files. The visualizer reads and syncs with the repo, so your ideas are version-controlled and portable.

Visual Mapping

Ideas that share more meaning sit closer together on the map. The layout emerges from semantic similarity, not manual positioning.

Private by Default

Microsoft (Azure AD) authentication restricts access to the configured owner. No browser storage. No anonymous usage. No data leaving the garden.

How It Works

The Workflow

1. Capture Add an idea with a title and brief description
2. Store Saved as markdown in your GitHub repo
3. Connect AI analyzes relationships between ideas
4. Discover Map view reveals unexpected connections

The Views

Map Spatial layout of semantic proximity
List Chronological, grouped, or filtered
Active Recently captured, not yet developed
Archive Mature ideas ready for reference

A Contemplative Interface

The interface is deliberately minimal—nothing competes with the work of thinking. There are no gamification loops, no streaks, no social feeds. The map view is the central surface: a field of ideas where proximity encodes meaning and distance encodes difference. Click an idea and you read it in full; hover and a summary surfaces the threads that tie it to the rest of your garden.

This is not a note-taking app in the traditional sense. It does not ask you to choose folders or notebooks. It does not demand that you decide upfront how ideas relate. Instead, it watches as you add thoughts and, over time, surfaces the connections you did not know you were making.

Why a Private Garden?

Privacy as a Feature

Your ideas are yours. No model training, no data brokerage, no attention economy. Local AI keeps the analysis in your hands.

Portability

Markdown on GitHub means you are never locked in. Leave at any time with your full history intact.

Ownership

You control the domain, the server, and the keys. The tool serves you, not a platform's agenda.

Emergent Structure

Structure emerges from content, not from upfront taxonomies. Let the AI find the seams in your thinking.
